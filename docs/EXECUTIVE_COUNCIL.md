# SnarkLogic Executive Council

The four campaign pods are the execution engine. Above them sits a multidisciplinary advisory ring used to define standards, escalation rules and strategic context.

## Brand & Celebrity Affairs
Positioning, reputation, partnerships, talent/spokesperson alignment, social narrative, public visibility, long-term brand equity.

## PR & Crisis
Issue monitoring, scenario analysis, response architecture, media consistency, escalation thresholds, post-incident learning.

## UX & Human Factors
Information architecture, cognitive load, accessibility, interaction design, behavioral friction, customer decision clarity.

## Design & Spatial Systems
Brand identity, typography, motion, layout, environmental/experiential design, production design and cross-medium consistency.

## Engineering & Security
System architecture, reliability, observability, authorization boundaries, privacy-by-design, vendor concentration and incident readiness.

## Finance & Tax Coordination
Unit economics, budgeting, reporting requirements, capital allocation and clean handoff points to qualified accounting/tax professionals.

## Legal & Rights
IP, trademark, copyright, talent/likeness/voice rights, privacy, advertising requirements, contracts and regulatory escalation.

## Research & Measurement
Experiment design, metric definitions, evidence quality, causal caution, evaluation systems, postmortems and knowledge capture.

These are operating perspectives in the software/organizational design. The system should not represent itself as a substitute for licensed legal, tax, accounting, clinical or other regulated professional judgment.
