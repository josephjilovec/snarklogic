# SnarkLogic Brand Strategy

## Core position
SnarkLogic is a comedic advertising operating system: creative taste, behavioral intelligence, talent infrastructure, risk controls, and performance feedback in one loop.

## Tagline
**Make them laugh. Make the numbers move.**

## Brand duality
- **SNARK** = surprise, absurdity, specificity, cultural fluency, self-awareness.
- **LOGIC** = evidence, repeatability, unit economics, rights lineage, governance, experiments.

Neither half works alone. Snark without logic is a liability. Logic without snark is another optimized ad people skip.

## Voice
1. Dry, not try-hard.
2. Specific, not corporate.
3. Provocative, not careless.
4. Data-literate, not robotic.
5. Self-aware, never self-important.

## Copy rules
- Prefer concrete nouns and specific situations over vague marketing language.
- Jokes should sharpen a product truth or customer tension.
- Do not manufacture performance claims.
- Do not target protected classes or vulnerable people as the butt of the joke.
- Do not confuse hostility with wit.
- Use jargon only when speaking to an audience that actually uses it.

## Visual identity
- Obsidian `#0B0B0C`: intelligence, seriousness, contrast.
- Warm Paper `#F4F0E7`: editorial humanity; prevents the brand from feeling like a cold AI dashboard.
- Signal Lime `#C8FF3D`: analytics, active state, positive system signal.
- Hot Coral `#FF655C`: comedic heat, risk, interruption.
- Electric Violet `#7765FF`: synthetic depth and future-facing moments.
- Graphite `#242428`: secondary dark surface.

## Design principles
- Editorial typography over glossy SaaS gradients.
- Visible borders and grids make the "logic" layer tangible.
- Controlled asymmetry communicates the "snark" layer.
- Use data-system visuals, not robot imagery.
- Large typography should carry the humor; decoration stays subordinate.
