# SnarkLogic — 10-Slide Investor Pitch Framework

## 1. The Contrarian Thesis
Advertising got optimized until it became ignorable. Generative production creates more content, making differentiated taste and learning systems more valuable.

## 2. The Pain
Brands face creative fatigue, fragmented workflows, slow production, inconsistent rights context, expensive testing, and weak institutional memory about what actually worked.

## 3. The Product
A comedic advertising operating system spanning behavioral insight, creative generation, human talent, rights/risk review, production, experiment design and performance feedback.

## 4. The Wedge
Start in high-velocity short-form performance creative, where faster concept cycles and clearer angle-level learning can be measured quickly.

## 5. Why Now
Generative media lowered asset-production cost. The bottleneck is shifting toward taste, governance, rights, structured experimentation, and data quality.

## 6. The Multi-Agent Moat
Specialist agents create structured disagreement and capture expert reasoning as reusable institutional memory. The moat is the evolving decision/performance graph, not merely model access.

## 7. Business Model
- B2B SaaS subscriptions / usage
- creator marketplace take rate
- enterprise studio retainers/production
- performance-aligned service structures where appropriate
- future creative-intelligence/API licensing

## 8. Economics
Software provides the scalable margin layer; services and marketplace activity can seed proprietary workflow/performance data and enterprise relationships. Present validated metrics only once real cohorts exist.

## 9. Expansion
Creative OS → creator/talent infrastructure → cross-channel allocation intelligence → adaptive brand systems → conversational brand agents.

## 10. The Ask
Capital allocation across product, data infrastructure, creator network, enterprise sales, compliance/governance, and measured distribution experiments.
