# Snark Logic Brand Architecture

## Master brand

**Snark Logic**

**Tagline:** Comedy systems for feeds and boardrooms.

Snark Logic is one creative-intelligence company with two product surfaces that share a structured-disagreement engine but have different users, objectives, data boundaries, and risk thresholds.

## Public engine — Creator R&D

Audience:
- independent digital creators
- meme and culture pages
- content studios
- entertainment brands

Jobs:
- hook generation
- timing analysis
- humor-tolerance mapping
- narrative subversion
- retention-oriented creative review
- creator-specific pattern memory

Commercial model:
- self-serve creator plans
- studio/network tiers
- higher-volume content R&D workflows

## Private engine — Enterprise Communications OS

Audience:
- corporate communications
- PR and reputation teams
- brand/social teams
- internal communications / people teams

Jobs:
- tone translation
- cringe detection
- backlash and context-collapse testing
- satire/wit tolerance controls
- escalation and human review gates
- organization-specific approval memory

Commercial model:
- private pilots
- custom enterprise deployment
- communication stress-testing retainers
- organization-specific workflow and guardrail design

## Shared engine

Four lenses:
1. Behavioral Intelligence
2. Cultural Signal
3. Creative & Narrative
4. Risk & Anti-Fragility

The point is not to have software imitate a room full of job titles. The point is to force important creative and communication tradeoffs into explicit, challengeable roles.

## Brand separation from advertising platforms

Snark Logic should not be positioned around media spend, CPA, ROAS, ad-network optimization, or campaign-budget allocation. Its product lives upstream of distribution: idea quality, hook structure, timing, audience tolerance, communication tone, narrative surprise, and downside testing.

## Visual system

- Void — `#090909`
- Warm paper — `#F3EFE6`
- Creator heat — `#FF5D2E`
- Acid signal — `#E8FF6A`
- Enterprise ice — `#8EDCFF`
- Lavender — `#B6A9FF`
- Graphite — `#202023`

The visual contrast mirrors the company structure: creator-facing surfaces feel hot, fast, editorial, and culturally alive; enterprise surfaces feel cool, controlled, legible, and defensive. The master brand remains black/paper so neither side swallows the other.
