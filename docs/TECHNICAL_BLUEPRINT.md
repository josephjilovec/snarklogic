# SnarkLogic Technical Workflow Blueprint

## 1. Client intake
Capture: product truth, audience, offer, economics, claims, brand constitution, channel constraints, current creative, competitors, rights limitations, success criteria.

## 2. Structured campaign object
Normalize the brief into machine- and human-readable fields. Keep source links/attachments and confidence notes attached to claims.

## 3. Behavioral mapping
Behavioral pod produces resistance hypotheses, audience language patterns, emotional tensions, humor tolerance, and category fatigue observations.

## 4. Cultural signal pass
Capture format/meme/trend candidates with timestamp, platform, expected half-life, and brand-fit score. Fast-moving signals should expire automatically instead of becoming permanent brand rules.

## 5. Creative generation
Generate concept families with mechanism tags: misdirection, specificity, rule-of-three, escalation, contrast, self-deprecation, visual hyperbole, character, anti-ad confession, etc.

Each concept object should contain: hook, product truth, comedic mechanism, script, storyboard, required assets, talent need, claims, variants, and intended audience/channel.

## 6. Agent challenge round
- psychology challenges comprehension/tone
- finance challenges testability/economics
- risk challenges claims/IP/policy/reputation
- creative responds and revises

Preserve disagreements rather than flattening them into one unexplained score.

## 7. Human approval gates
Required for legal/rights decisions, material spend commitments, high-severity brand risk, regulated claims, synthetic likeness/voice usage, and other designated policies.

## 8. Production routing
Choose human creator, studio shoot, motion design, generative video, synthetic VO, hybrid, localization, or template versioning. Every asset inherits the rights/claim context of the concept.

## 9. Experiment matrix
Define audience, placement, creative ID, angle ID, hook ID, offer, spend tier, expected signal, stop condition, and success metric before launch.

## 10. Media data ingestion
Import platform outcomes into normalized event/performance tables. Do not let platform naming become your internal ontology.

## 11. Learning loop
Aggregate findings at multiple layers: asset, hook, angle, mechanism, audience, creator, channel, offer. Store uncertainty and sample size with conclusions.

## 12. Capital recommendation
Finance pod proposes budget changes based on marginal performance and confidence. Human operators retain authorization boundaries.
